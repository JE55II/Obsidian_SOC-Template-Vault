The modifications (Graph widget, ticket heatmaps, removal of author-specific sources, new plugin id) are released with the fork under **GPL-3.0**. The upstream MIT notice is kept in the fork's `LICENSE-MIT`. Complete corresponding source: <https://github.com/JE55II/custom_aurora-dashboard>.
